ALTTEZ predictive engine

ALTTEZ turns free public data into win probabilities for international football and for NBA games. It scores every model walk-forward against simple baselines on the same games, and it grades its own live predictions after the games end. It keeps the results that went against its own models.

Every number on this site is read from a data file generated for this build. Lower Brier is better.

Honest findings

  • The legacy Poisson soccer model lost to plain Elo. An audit found it worse than an Elo-only baseline pooled and in most test years. A replacement rule was chosen on earlier years and then tested on years it never saw. See the soccer page.
  • NBA Elo does not beat the closing line. It beats simpler baselines, but it does not beat the market close in any test season. So the served prediction defers to the market-based blend when a line exists. See the NBA page.
  • Negative results stay in the repository. The method page lists the leakage and duplication bugs the audit found and fixed.
