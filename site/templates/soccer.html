What this is not

  • It is not betting advice. Nobody should stake money on it.
  • It is the Elo ordinal rule only. It rates teams from results, so it knows nothing about injuries, squads or lineups.
  • The live pipeline can only predict fixtures that exist. National teams play in international windows, so coverage of live fixtures depends on the calendar.
